Questions I get asked

Do I need to buy anything else?

A domain name if you want a .co.ke or .com, about KSh 1,000 a year, and nothing more. Hosting is included in the monthly up-keep.

Can I change the prices myself later?

Yes. On the KSh 12,000 and 20,000 sites you get a simple page where you edit prices and photos with no code. On the KSh 6,000 page you send me the change and I do it within the month.

Will it work when my customers' bundles run out?

The page is small and caches itself, so a customer who opened it once can open it again with no data. Photos are compressed hard on purpose.

What if I stop paying the KSh 500?

The site stays up with your content, you keep the files and the domain. You just do your own changes and hosting renewal from then on.

Do you take M-Pesa?

Yes, Pochi la Biashara. You get a receipt message for every payment. Half before the build, half on approval.
